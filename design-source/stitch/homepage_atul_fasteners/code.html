Who We Are

Kartikey Fasteners (starting with background of Girdhar Fastners established in 1999, then Kartikey Fasteners in 2018) is a premier manufacturer of high-tensile industrial fasteners, forged through decades of metallurgical expertise and rigorous quality control. We do not just manufacture components; we engineer the critical joints that hold modern infrastructure together.

Our state-of-the-art facilities leverage advanced cold forging technologies and precision machining to produce standardized and custom fasteners that meet the exacting demands of the automotive, construction, and heavy machinery sectors globally.
